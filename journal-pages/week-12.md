### Wind Chime Garden



Wind Chime Garden is an interactive data visualisation that transforms everyday food records into a living digital forest. Rather than presenting health information through traditional graphs, charts, or calorie counters, this project visualises eating habits as personalised wind chimes hanging from growing trees.

The project is based on food diary data collected over several weeks, including daily meal records, estimated calorie intake, and self-reported energy levels. Each wind chime represents one day of eating. Individual pendants represent breakfast, lunch, dinner, and other, while the tree itself grows over time as more data is recorded. Together, the wind chimes form a visual archive of personal eating habits and wellbeing. This work explores how data can move beyond numbers and become something emotional, reflective, and meaningful. Inspired by data physicalisation, health-tracking technologies, and personal journaling practices, Wind Chime Garden challenges conventional approaches to health data representation. Rather than focusing on optimisation, targets, or performance metrics, it encourages users to reflect on their relationship with food and self-care through visual storytelling.

Many health-tracking applications reduce people to numbers, calorie counts, and performance metrics. While these systems can provide useful information, they often encourage users to focus on optimisation rather than reflection. Wind Chime Garden challenges this approach by transforming food data into memories, relationships, and acts of care. Instead of treating eating as a problem to solve, the project invites users to reflect on the routines, experiences, and circumstances that shape their eating habits. Through the visual language of wind chimes, trees, and a shared community forest, the project reimagines food data as something personal, emotional, and socially connected. Rather than measuring success through targets or achievements, it encourages awareness, reflection, and mutual care.

The project imagines a future where food data becomes part of a supportive community rather than a private record. Users can invite and view the forests of friends and family members, encouraging mutual awareness and care. In a society where busy schedules often lead people to skip meals and neglect their wellbeing, the project proposes a different role for data: not as a tool for surveillance or competition, but as a reminder to care for ourselves and the people around us. 

In Wind Chime Garden, data is no longer presented as charts or statistics. Instead, it grows into trees, hangs as wind chimes, and accumulates as memories. Each meal, each day, and each wind chime becomes part of a living archive that records not only what we eat, but also the routines, relationships, and experiences that shape our wellbeing. By imagining a shared forest of personal food diaries, the project explores how data might become a tool for reflection, connection, and care rather than simply measurement and monitoring. Ultimately, Wind Chime Garden transforms everyday food records into a growing landscape of memory, self-reflection, and human connection.
