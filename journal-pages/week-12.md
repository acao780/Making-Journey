There are three assessed elements:

Process Documentation
You will continue documenting your project on your GitHub Pages website using the provided template. This includes recording experiments, technical learning, conceptual development, and engagement with peer critique and exchange activities.

Final Artefact
Your final data-driven visualisation, intended for public display. This can take various forms: screen-based animations or interactive applications; 3D-printed or found/fabricated physical objects; 2D paper-based works; or other formats appropriate to your project. Your work must be suitable for display in the showcase and documented on your website.

Project Statement
A 300-word statement for a public audience that introduces your work. This should address: what the visualisation is, the data source(s) used, the subject matter and future scenario, how the work engages critically with ideas about data representation, and the intended impact of the work. The statement should function as a ‘wall text’ to provide viewers with insight into your intentions and approach.
